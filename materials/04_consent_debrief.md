# Informed Consent & Debrief

## Informed Consent (before the survey)

**Study Title:** Consumer decision-making with AI shopping assistants

**What you'll do:** You will review information about two products, see a
recommendation from an AI shopping assistant, make a choice, and answer
questions about your experience. Takes about 8–12 minutes.

**Voluntary:** Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time.

**Anonymity:** We do not collect names or identifying information. Your
MTurk Worker ID is used only to issue payment and is not linked to your
responses in analysis.

**Risks/Benefits:** Minimal risk, no greater than everyday online activity.

**Payment:** You will receive the stated reward upon submitting a valid
completion code.

☐ I am 18+ and consent to participate.

---

## Debrief (must be read after the survey — because it involves benign deception)

Thank you for participating. We want to be fully transparent about the
study's design:

The "AI shopping assistant" in this study intentionally recommended the
**objectively weaker laptop**. In reality, Laptop A was the better value
on every dimension (price, CPU, memory, battery, warranty). The
recommendation you saw was designed by the researchers, not a real AI
product, and its supporting "sources" were part of the experimental design.

**Why the deception?** We are studying *verification theater* — how the
mere appearance of citations (even unverifiable or misleading ones) shapes
trust in AI systems and the quality of people's decisions. We could not
measure this without keeping the true purpose hidden during the task.

Your responses help us understand how people can be protected from
misplaced trust in AI recommendations. If you have questions or want your
data withdrawn, contact: [researcher email].

Thank you for helping with this research.
